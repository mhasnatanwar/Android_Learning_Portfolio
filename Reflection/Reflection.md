# Final Reflection - Student Authored

The assessment requires your own reflection and ability to explain the work. Use the prompts below, remove the prompts, and write a coherent reflection in your own voice.

## Learning progression

- What could you build or explain after Module 4 that you could not after Module 1?
- Which connection between Kotlin, Compose state, architecture, navigation, and adaptive UI became clear?

## Challenges and solutions

- Describe one real technical challenge.
- What did you try first, what evidence showed it was not enough, and what finally worked?

## Technical decisions

- Choose two decisions from the code.
- What alternatives did you consider, and why was the selected approach suitable here?

## Version control practice

- How did small commits or repository organization help you track and explain the work?
- Be honest about any limitations in the timeline.

## Next learning goal

- Name one specific Android skill you will develop next and how you will verify progress.
